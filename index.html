How it works

  • 1Each round asks a tricky "how many…?" question with three facts (anchors) to reason from.
  • 2You don't answer with one number — you drag a range. A tighter range scores more, but only if the real answer is inside it. Too wide barely scores; too tight and you risk missing and losing points.
  • 3One free check per round tests the anchor you trust least. Some are planted traps that look sourced — checking costs nothing, trusting a bad one costs the plan.
  • 4Eight rounds. On one, the boss wants a single number — commit for a big payout, or play it safe with a range.
